About Hop Along Books

We make color by number books for children ages 3-5 - and we measure every page.

Most coloring books put an age range on the cover and hope. We started Hop Along Books because the books we found for 3-year-olds had spaces too small, too many colors, and keys that showed only dots.

So every Hop Along book follows the same rules:

  • 5 levels, easy to harder - from 3 colors and inch-wide shapes up to 8-color scenes.
  • Every space is measured. Level 1 spaces are about an inch wide; even Level 5 keeps every space about half an inch or bigger.
  • Color names on every key, in everyday colors you already own.
  • One picture per page, with a fact, a color test strip, a feelings face and a drawing box on the back.
  • Answer pictures at the back, and a small color example on every page.

The books are published through Amazon KDP and printed on demand. Line art and text are produced with AI tools and checked by hand, page by page.
